# Deck Rhythm

Plan density and visual role across the deck. Cover and closing are low-density focal pages. Explanation pages are medium density. Data pages are medium-high density. Section pages reset rhythm.

`slides_plan.json` must include `density`, `visual_role`, `focal_point`, `contrast_with_previous`, `repeated_motif`, and `section_color_state` when Phase 3 design is active.
