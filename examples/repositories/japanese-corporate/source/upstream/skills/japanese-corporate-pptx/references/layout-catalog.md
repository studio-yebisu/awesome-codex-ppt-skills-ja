# Layout Catalog

The bundled template contains 15 editable source slides.

## Slide 1 — 講演・報告タイトル＋進行

Use for industry-association lectures, committee reports, technical briefings, and formal workshop openings.

Contains a compact title, date, delivery format, speaker, and audience-facing agenda.

## Slide 2 — 主張＋根拠＋注記

Use for executive position statements, issue framing, key messages with caveats, and section introductions.

Contains large bullets, emphasized phrases, a definition or boundary, and a bottom evidence box.

## Slide 3 — 人物・会社・事業紹介

Use for speaker profiles, company introductions, program teams, and business portfolios.

Contains one identity zone, business/history text, two documentary photos, captions, and proof points.

## Slide 4 — 年代・技術動向

Use for technology evolution, market history, policy timelines, and project maturity.

Contains a colored chevron band, multiple swimlanes, named events, current point, future callout, and notes.

## Slide 5 — 技術・産業レイヤー

Use for system stacks, industry stacks, organization layers, governance layers, and value chains.

Contains five thick outlined central layers, side enablers, applications, and an intervention highlight.

## Slide 6 — 関係者・価値交換

Use for platforms, ecosystems, business models, governance, data and money flow, and partnerships.

Contains a central mechanism, surrounding actors, labelled arrows, feedback loop, risks, and enablers.

## Slide 7 — 事業・政策スキーム

Use for government programs, subsidies, industry initiatives, project charters, and budget summaries.

Contains purpose, target, content, execution structure, outcome, conditions, and responsible owner.

## Slide 8 — 業務プロセス＋役割

Use for current or future processes, responsibility handoffs, operating models, and implementation flows.

Contains stages across the top, role swimlanes, decisions, systems, pain points, and output.

## Slide 9 — 現状→変革→目指す姿

Use for transformation proposals, strategy maps, change programs, and operating-model redesign.

Contains current state, intervention, future state, enabling foundation, metrics, and constraints.

## Slide 10 — ロードマップ＋判断ゲート

Use for phased implementation, maturity progression, research plans, and standardization paths.

Contains phases, milestones, owners, dependencies, risks, decision gates, and target state.

## Slide 11 — データ・引用図表＋解釈

Use for market forecasts, source charts, survey results, investment trends, and technical benchmarks.

Contains a native chart or bounded official-source excerpt, direct annotations, three implications, caveat, and action.

## Slide 12 — 比較・判断

Use for option selection, vendor comparison, risk-response matrices, before/after views, and recommendations.

Contains an exact comparison table, highlighted recommendation, remaining risk, adoption condition, and next action.

## Slide 13 — 原典抜粋・比較・読み解き

Use when two official figures, PDF pages, charts, or external references must be compared without losing provenance.

Contains two bounded excerpt frames, red focus boxes, captions, source-page fields, processing notes, commonality, difference, and decision implication.

## Slide 14 — 論点・対応方針・継続審議

Use for working groups, steering committees, management reviews, project issue logs, and approval meetings.

Contains material number, meeting date, assumptions, a dense issue-and-response table, owner, due date, status, decision items, and continuing discussion.

## Slide 15 — 引用グラフ・時点注釈・結論

Use when one official or third-party chart is the central evidence and the presenter must show the conclusion, turning point, future threshold, and business implication directly on the figure.

Contains a left evidence chart or source-image frame, a separate right-side interpretation rail, editable time or threshold markers, an independent conclusion block, processing and provenance notes, and a bottom decision implication. The composition intentionally avoids tracing any single reference page.

## Selection rule

Choose the layout whose relationship structure matches the content:

- time relationship → slide 4 or 10;
- hierarchy → slide 5;
- stakeholder exchange → slide 6;
- policy or funding flow → slide 7;
- role and sequence → slide 8;
- transformation → slide 9;
- evidence → slide 11;
- exact decision → slide 12;
- official excerpts and their interpretation → slide 13;
- issue ownership and meeting closure → slide 14.
- one dominant source chart with time or threshold callouts → slide 15.
