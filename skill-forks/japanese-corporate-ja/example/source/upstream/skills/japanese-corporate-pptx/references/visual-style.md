# Visual Style

## Target character

Aim for:

- formal, technical, and explanatory;
- information-rich but visibly ordered;
- one dominant model with surrounding evidence;
- specific actors, arrows, labels, dates, units, and sources;
- editable PowerPoint objects;
- a page that can support discussion without oral explanation filling every gap.

Avoid polishing away the logic. The style may be visually busy, but it must not be structurally ambiguous.

## Canvas and furniture

- Canvas: 1280 × 720, 16:9.
- Top rail: 48–58px, usually full-width dark blue.
- Body: y=68–678.
- Body margins: 24–54px.
- Footer rail or organization mark: 28–36px.
- Page number: lower right.
- Source: directly above the footer or at the lower edge of the evidence area.
- Main occupancy: 75–90%.
- Document-control badge: upper-right of the white body or inside the rail, 100–260px wide.

Use a stable reading order:

1. topic rail;
2. conclusion title or highlighted thesis;
3. dominant figure;
4. evidence and annotations;
5. implication, caveat, and source.

## Palette

| Role | Default |
|---|---|
| JEITA-like rail blue | `#0B5AA6` |
| Deep navy | `#092B4C` |
| Structural blue | `#2474B5` |
| Bright cyan | `#00A9E0` |
| Pale blue | `#DDEBF7` |
| Teal | `#009C95` |
| Green target | `#75B843` |
| Pale green | `#E2F0D9` |
| Orange attention | `#ED7D31` |
| Red risk/current | `#C00000` |
| Gray note | `#E7E6E6` |
| Body black | `#111111` |
| White | `#FFFFFF` |

The base is blue, white, and black. Use cyan, green, orange, or red to encode meaning. Do not use all accents merely for decoration.

## Typography

Preferred:

1. `BIZ UDPゴシック`
2. `Yu Gothic`
3. `Meiryo`
4. `Arial` for English labels

Recommended tiers:

| Element | Size |
|---|---|
| Cover title | 38–50pt |
| Rail title | 24–32pt |
| Main conclusion | 24–34pt |
| Diagram entity | 17–24pt |
| Primary body | 16–20pt |
| Supporting explanation | 13–16pt |
| Dense reference detail | 11–13pt |
| Source and caveat | 8–10pt |

Compact tiers are allowed because this style is explicitly template-led. Keep them limited to evidence, annotations, and source text.

Use bold, blue, underline, or red emphasis on short phrases. Do not underline entire paragraphs.

## Shape language

Prefer:

- square rectangles;
- outlined layer blocks with 2–4px strokes;
- chevrons and block arrows;
- straight or elbow connectors;
- horizontal swimlanes;
- simple ellipses for actors or milestones;
- flat gray evidence boxes;
- dotted or dashed boundaries for scope;
- braces or side labels for grouped layers.
- compact upper-right material-number boxes;
- red outline callouts around a bounded source area;
- small legends that explain line color, status, or actual/forecast boundaries.

Corner radius should normally be 0–4px. Avoid shadows unless inherited from a reference.

Create connectors before entity nodes. Keep arrow labels adjacent to, not on top of, arrowheads.

## Layout signatures

### JEITA timeline

- full-width blue rail;
- colored chevron band across the top third;
- two to four long horizontal arrows beneath;
- company, model, or technology names positioned directly on lanes;
- a red or cyan future callout;
- dense notes at the bottom.

### Layer cake

- five to seven thick outlined horizontal layers;
- inputs and enabling assets on the left;
- outputs and applications on the right;
- one highlighted intervention layer;
- explanatory paragraph below.

### Ministry scheme

- top-left purpose and target;
- top-right outcome or numerical goal;
- lower half execution diagram;
- arrows labelled with subsidy, data, contract, product, or responsibility;
- responsible division, budget, source, and caveat.

### Stakeholder ecosystem

- central mechanism or platform;
- four to six surrounding actors;
- separate arrows for data, money, goods, decisions, and feedback;
- side notes for governance, risks, and enablers.

### Source-excerpt working paper

- one or two large bounded source frames;
- source title and page directly beneath each frame;
- red focus box, arrow, or `要確認` note;
- processing note such as `公開資料を一部加工` when true;
- bottom strip that states commonality, difference, and decision implication.

### Annotated source chart

- one chart or bounded source image occupies roughly 55–70% of the page;
- a separate interpretation rail explains change, cause, and decision;
- one or two red dotted vertical lines mark actual dates, turning points, or thresholds;
- a conclusion block appears outside the plot in a newly designed position;
- the business or policy implication closes the page in a distinct decision strip;
- the source line and processing note remain outside the plot;
- original chart labels remain visually distinct from presenter-added annotations.
- the surrounding composition must not near-trace a single reference page.

### Issue-and-response table

- upper-right material number and status;
- one compact assumptions strip above the table;
- columns for issue, fact, response, owner, due date, and status;
- status colors used semantically;
- separate bottom zones for decisions and continuing discussion.

## Images

Use images as evidence or operating context:

- actual training, facility, field, or product photography;
- official source charts or diagrams;
- verified portrait or organization photo;
- generated fictional image only for a clearly illustrative use.

Image treatment:

- one to three images per page;
- crop to the relevant subject;
- add a 1–2px gray or blue border;
- add a compact caption;
- add source when external;
- do not reuse the same image on multiple slides;
- do not use faux-polaroid, circular portrait clusters, or decorative stock-photo mosaics.

## Avoid

- startup-pitch hero slogans;
- extreme whitespace;
- repeated rounded cards;
- dashboard widgets, pills, tabs, or fake UI;
- neon purple gradients;
- decorative AI brains or holograms;
- icon walls without relationships;
- 3D diagrams;
- unlabeled arrows;
- cleanliness that removes actors, exceptions, or evidence.
