component chart on page space (5 of 6): content, kind "hierarchy"
name: Plot
what it is: Numbers drawn as a shape: bars, stacked or 100% columns, bars under a line, a line, an area, slices, a funnel, a from-and-to pair, a point cloud, or one arc against a target.
choose it: Choose it when the audience should grasp the shape of the numbers at a glance. Use data_table when exact values must be read row by row, and comparison when the attributes are words rather than figures.
not for: Several independent headline figures, which belong in kpi_cards.
full-body: no
limits on this page:
  at most 8 series in each chart (line and area charts, past which the lines stop reading apart), validate warns past it
