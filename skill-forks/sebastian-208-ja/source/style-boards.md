## 共通プロンプト
Create one polished visual-direction preview board for a Japanese image-based academic design proposal, based on academic-image-ppt's preview-board grammar. Topic: 「雨の図書室」, subtitle 「街の雨を読む、小さな観測拠点」. This is a fictional spatial-design research proposal, not an existing building or completed study. Do not generate the final deck in this call.

The board has a wide introduction header, exactly six miniature slides in a clear two-row grid, and a bottom design specification band. All six miniature slides must be horizontal true 16:9 frames with identical aspect ratios. Do not make square cards, tall cards, stretched slides, cropped frames, or rotated slides. The whole board may be a different aspect ratio if needed for legibility.

Header exact text: 「雨の図書室」「架空の空間デザイン研究提案」 and the variant title specified below.
Six miniatures:
1. Cover: 「雨の図書室」 / 「街の雨を読む、小さな観測拠点」. Show a fictional small shelter in rain with a roof, seat and record shelf. Big editorial title, asymmetric layout.
2. Contents: 「雨を、四つの視点で読む。」, four items 「視点 03–04」「空間 05–06」「体験 07–08」「検証 09–10」. Show real editorial index hierarchy.
3. Section divider: 「空間」「一つの庇に、三つの役割。」. Use one horizontal roof line and rain lines, not a generic title card.
4. Content: 「集める。聞く。残す。」. Show a large architectural concept section with exact callout labels 「雨粒を受ける庇」「音を聞く席」「記録を置く棚」 and small qualifier 「概念図・寸法未設定」. These are concepts, not engineering drawings.
5. Content: 「何を見るかを、先に決める。」. Show an open three-stage conceptual cycle labeled 「試作する」「観察する」「見直す」 and qualifier 「検証計画案／実施前」. No charts or measured results.
6. Standalone closing: 「あなたの街なら、」「どこに置く？」 / 「質疑・対話」. One small shelter silhouette and strong negative space, no summary panel.

Each miniature should look like a real slide page with realistic text hierarchy and sufficient evidence-carrier detail. The board must not be six decorative covers. Keep all Japanese wording accurate and brief. Do not add university names, student names, advisors, dates, fake logos, locations, numerical outcomes, citations, measurements, budgets, crowd counts, or experimental findings. Do not use glossy real estate advertising imagery. All visuals are invented design concepts. Readability comes before decoration. No watermark. No random English filler.
The bottom band must include the exact Japanese section labels 「色」「書体」「図」「余白」「用途」 with the variant-specific descriptions below. Typography descriptions indicate intended visual character, not verified font embedding.
